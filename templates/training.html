Awareness Guide
Recognizing Phishing Attempts

Phishing messages often attempt to create urgency or encourage users to perform an unexpected action. Careful verification can reduce the risk of successful attacks.

1. Check the Sender

Review the sender address carefully. A familiar display name does not necessarily mean that the message is legitimate.

2. Review the Request

Be cautious when a message requests an unexpected password change, payment, download or other sensitive action.

3. Inspect Links Carefully

Before following a link, verify that the destination corresponds to the expected organization or service.

4. Do Not Act Under Pressure

Urgency is commonly used to influence users into making decisions without verification.

5. Report Suspicious Messages

When a message appears suspicious, report it through the organization's approved security reporting process.

Security Checklist
  • Verify the sender.
  • Check unexpected requests.
  • Review links before opening them.
  • Avoid acting under unnecessary pressure.
  • Report suspicious activity.
